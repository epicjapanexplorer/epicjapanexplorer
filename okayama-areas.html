Prefecture Area Guide

Okayama by Area

Okayama Prefecture breaks down into a handful of distinct areas, each with its own character. Click a pin on the map, or scroll down, to explore each one.

Where each area sits within Okayama
Okayama Prefecture's real boundary (same map data as the main site) — click a pin to jump to that area.
Okayama CityGarden & Black Castle

The prefectural capital, home to Korakuen — one of Japan's three great gardens — beside a striking black-walled castle.

  • Korakuen — one of Japan's three great landscape gardens.
  • Okayama Castle, nicknamed "Crow Castle" for its black exterior.
  • A walkable riverside city center.
KurashikiCanal Warehouse Town

A beautifully preserved canal town of white-walled warehouses, now home to galleries, cafes, and a renowned Western art museum.

  • Bikan Historical Quarter — preserved canal-side warehouses.
  • Ohara Museum of Art, one of Japan's first Western art museums.
  • Boat rides along the willow-lined canal.
BizenAncient Pottery Town

A historic pottery town famous for Bizen-yaki, one of Japan's oldest and most distinctive unglazed ceramic traditions.

  • Bizen-yaki — unglazed stoneware with a centuries-old tradition.
  • Numerous working kilns and pottery studios open to visitors.
  • One of Japan's "Six Ancient Kilns."
HiruzenHighland Dairy Country

A cool volcanic highland area known for dairy farms, hiking, and a relaxed alpine atmosphere.

  • Dairy farms producing fresh milk, cheese, and ice cream.
  • Cool highland hiking trails.
  • A relaxed, family-friendly atmosphere.
