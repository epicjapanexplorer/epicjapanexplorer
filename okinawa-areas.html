Prefecture Area Guide

Okinawa by Area

Okinawa Prefecture breaks down into a handful of distinct areas, each with its own character. Click a pin on the map, or scroll down, to explore each one.

Where each area sits within Okinawa
Okinawa Prefecture's real boundary (same map data as the main site) — click a pin to jump to that area.
Naha & Southern OkinawaCapital & Historic Castle

The prefectural capital, home to Shuri Castle and World War II historical sites in the island's southern reaches.

  • Shuri Castle, once the seat of the Ryukyu Kingdom.
  • Kokusai-dori, Naha's lively main shopping street.
  • Peace memorial sites in the island's south.
Central OkinawaBases, Pottery & Beaches

A region blending American military base culture with traditional pottery villages and popular beach resorts.

  • Yomitan and other traditional Ryukyu pottery villages.
  • American-influenced neighborhoods near the military bases.
  • Popular beach resort areas along the west coast.
Northern Okinawa (Yanbaru)Subtropical Forest & Aquarium

A subtropical forest region and UNESCO World Heritage site, home to rare wildlife and the Churaumi Aquarium.

  • Yanbaru's UNESCO World Heritage subtropical forest.
  • Churaumi Aquarium, one of the world's largest.
  • Rare wildlife found nowhere else, like the Okinawa rail.
Miyako IslandStunning White Sand Beaches

A remote island known for some of Japan's most stunning white sand beaches and brilliantly clear turquoise water.

  • Widely regarded as having some of Japan's best beaches.
  • Brilliantly clear turquoise water, ideal for swimming.
  • Connected to nearby islands by scenic bridges.
Ishigaki & Yaeyama IslandsWorld-Class Diving

A southern island group offering world-class diving, including coral reefs and mangroves, near the jungle-covered Iriomote Island.

  • World-class coral reef diving and snorkeling.
  • Iriomote Island's dense, jungle-covered interior.
  • A remote, tropical southernmost corner of Japan.
