Prefecture Area Guide

Yamanashi by Area

Yamanashi Prefecture breaks down into a handful of distinct areas, each with its own character. Click a pin on the map, or scroll down, to explore each one.

Where each area sits within Yamanashi
Yamanashi Prefecture's real boundary (same map data as the main site) — click a pin to jump to that area.
Fuji Five LakesLakes at the Foot of Fuji

A cluster of scenic lakes at the base of Mt. Fuji, offering some of the mountain's most iconic reflected views and access to hiking and outdoor activities.

  • Iconic reflected views of Mt. Fuji over Lake Kawaguchi.
  • Access to Mt. Fuji's 5th Station and climbing trails.
  • Outdoor activities from boating to cycling around the lakes.
Kofu & ShosenkyoCapital & Scenic Gorge

The prefectural capital, gateway to Shosenkyo Gorge — one of Japan's most scenic ravines, lined with dramatic rock formations.

  • Shosenkyo Gorge — dramatic rock formations and waterfalls.
  • Takeda Shrine, dedicated to famed warlord Takeda Shingen.
  • A relaxed capital city surrounded by mountains.
KatsunumaJapan's Wine Country

Japan's premier wine region, home to numerous vineyards and wineries built around the Koshu grape.

  • Dozens of wineries open for tastings and tours.
  • The native Koshu grape, Japan's signature wine varietal.
  • Vineyard-covered hillsides against a mountain backdrop.
YatsugatakeHighland Retreat

A cool volcanic mountain range popular for hiking, highland farms, and a relaxed alpine atmosphere.

  • Cool summer temperatures and extensive hiking trails.
  • Highland farms and dairies dotting the plateau.
  • A quieter alternative to more crowded alpine resorts.
