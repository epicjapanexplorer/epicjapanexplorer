Prefecture Area Guide

Tokyo by Area

Tokyo Prefecture breaks down into a handful of distinct areas, each with its own character. Click a pin on the map, or scroll down, to explore each one.

Where each area sits within Tokyo
Tokyo Prefecture's real boundary (same map data as the main site) — click a pin to jump to that area.
Tokyo StationTokyo's Gateway

The city's grand red-brick railway gateway, steps from the Imperial Palace's moats, gardens, and the wide public plaza in front of it.

  • Tokyo Station's restored red-brick Marunouchi facade.
  • The Imperial Palace East Gardens, open to the public.
  • A central hub connecting to nearly everywhere in Japan.
AsakusaTokyo's Traditional Heart

Tokyo's most traditional district, centered on Sensoji Temple and the bustling Nakamise shopping street leading up to it.

  • Sensoji — Tokyo's oldest and most visited temple.
  • Nakamise-dori, a shopping street selling snacks and souvenirs.
  • Views of Tokyo Skytree from along the Sumida River.
GinzaLuxury Shopping District

Tokyo's flagship luxury shopping district, known for flagship department stores, high-end boutiques, and elegant dining.

  • Flagship stores from Japanese and international luxury brands.
  • Weekend pedestrian-only "Ginza Hokosha Tengoku" street closures.
  • Some of Tokyo's most storied department stores.
Shinjuku & ShibuyaSkyscrapers & Youth Culture

Tokyo's two most iconic hubs — Shinjuku for its skyscraper district and nightlife, Shibuya for its famous scramble crossing and youth culture.

  • Shinjuku's skyscraper district and the world's busiest railway station.
  • Shibuya Scramble Crossing — Tokyo's most famous intersection.
  • Dense nightlife, dining, and shopping in both districts.
OdaibaFuturistic Waterfront

A futuristic waterfront district on a man-made island in Tokyo Bay, with shopping malls, a giant Ferris wheel, and views of the Rainbow Bridge.

  • Views of the Rainbow Bridge and Tokyo Bay skyline.
  • TeamLab digital art museums and other tech-forward attractions.
  • A life-size Gundam statue and other pop-culture landmarks.
