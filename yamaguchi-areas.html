Prefecture Area Guide

Yamaguchi by Area

Yamaguchi Prefecture breaks down into a handful of distinct areas, each with its own character. Click a pin on the map, or scroll down, to explore each one.

Where each area sits within Yamaguchi
Yamaguchi Prefecture's real boundary (same map data as the main site) — click a pin to jump to that area.
Hagi & NagatoMeiji Restoration Castle Town

A preserved castle town tied to the Meiji Restoration, paired with the dramatic Sea of Japan coastline around Nagato.

  • A UNESCO-linked castle town tied to Japan's Meiji Restoration.
  • Preserved samurai residences and white-walled streets.
  • Nagato's dramatic nearby coastline, including Motonosumi Shrine's torii row.
Akiyoshidai & AkiyoshidoKarst Plateau & Cave

Japan's largest karst plateau above ground, with one of its largest limestone caves running beneath it.

  • Japan's largest karst plateau, dotted with limestone formations.
  • Akiyoshido, one of Japan's largest limestone caves.
  • Dramatic underground chambers and rock formations.
Shimonoseki & TsunoshimaPufferfish City & Blue Bridge

A historic strait-side city famous for fugu (pufferfish) cuisine, near the striking blue waters of Tsunoshima Bridge.

  • Fugu (pufferfish) cuisine, a Shimonoseki specialty.
  • Kanmon Strait views between Honshu and Kyushu.
  • Tsunoshima Bridge, spanning brilliant turquoise water.
IwakuniIconic Wooden Bridge

Home to Kintai-kyo, an elegant five-arched wooden bridge considered one of Japan's most beautiful.

  • Kintai-kyo — a graceful five-arched wooden bridge.
  • A hillside castle with views over the bridge and river.
  • Cherry blossoms along the riverbank in spring.
