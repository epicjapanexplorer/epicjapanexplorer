KusatsuHot Spring Town

One of Japan's most celebrated hot spring towns, centered on the steaming, sulfurous Yubatake ("hot water field") at its heart, with a centuries-old bathing culture.

  • Yubatake — a steaming hot-water field at the center of town.
  • Yumomi dance performances, a traditional cooling ritual for the hot spring water.
  • Consistently ranked among Japan's top hot spring destinations.
Ikaho & HarunaStone Stairway Onsen

A historic hot spring town famous for its 365-step stone stairway lined with shops, paired with the scenic volcanic Mt. Haruna and its crater lake nearby.

  • Ikaho's iconic 365-step stone stairway lined with ryokan and shops.
  • Mt. Haruna and Lake Haruna — a scenic volcanic crater lake nearby.
  • Reddish-brown "golden" hot spring water, said to be good for the skin.
MinakamiOutdoor Adventure

An outdoor adventure hub in the mountains, known for whitewater rafting, canyoning, and gorge walks, with rustic hot springs to unwind in afterward.

  • Whitewater rafting on the Tone River, some of Japan's best.
  • Canyoning and gorge-walking tours in summer.
  • Numerous rustic hot spring villages tucked into the mountains.
TomiokaUNESCO Silk Heritage

Home to the Tomioka Silk Mill, a UNESCO World Heritage site that helped launch Japan's modern silk industry in the 19th century.

  • Tomioka Silk Mill — a UNESCO World Heritage industrial site.
  • Japan's first modern silk-reeling factory, built in 1872.
  • Well-preserved brick architecture blending Japanese and Western styles.
