Shiga by Area

Shiga Prefecture breaks down into a handful of distinct areas, each with its own character. Click a pin on the map, or scroll down, to explore each one.

Where each area sits within Shiga
Shiga Prefecture's real boundary (same map data as the main site) — click a pin to jump to that area.
Mt. HieiUNESCO Sacred Mountain

Home to Enryaku-ji, a UNESCO World Heritage Buddhist monastery complex on a sacred mountain overlooking Lake Biwa.

  • Enryaku-ji — a UNESCO World Heritage monastery complex.
  • Sweeping views over Lake Biwa and Kyoto from the summit.
  • A founding center of several major schools of Japanese Buddhism.
HikoneOriginal Castle Town

A historic castle town centered on one of Japan's few original castles, with its beloved mascot Hikonyan.

  • Hikone Castle — one of Japan's few surviving original castles.
  • Hikonyan, the samurai-helmeted cat mascot, appears regularly.
  • A well-preserved castle-town atmosphere.
OmihachimanMerchant Canal Town

A preserved merchant town of canals and warehouses, once home to a powerful network of traveling Omi merchants.

  • Boat tours along the preserved Hachiman-bori canal.
  • A legacy of the influential traveling Omi merchant network.
  • White-walled warehouses along the waterways.
Lake BiwaJapan's Largest Lake

Japan's largest lake, ringed by mountains and popular for water sports, cycling, and scenic lakeside towns.

  • Japan's largest lake, a major water sports and cycling destination.
  • A popular round-lake cycling route (Biwaichi).
  • Scenic lakeside towns and mountain backdrops.
