Prefecture Area Guide

Ehime by Area

Ehime Prefecture breaks down into a handful of distinct areas, each with its own character. Click a pin on the map, or scroll down, to explore each one.

Where each area sits within Ehime
Ehime Prefecture's real boundary (same map data as the main site) — click a pin to jump to that area.
Matsuyama & DogoCastle & Ancient Onsen

The prefectural capital, home to one of Japan's finest original castles and Dogo Onsen, one of the country's oldest hot springs.

  • Matsuyama Castle — one of Japan's finest surviving original castles.
  • Dogo Onsen, among Japan's oldest documented hot springs.
  • Strong literary ties to novelist Natsume Soseki.
Imabari & Shimanami KaidoCycling Gateway

A shipbuilding city and the starting point of the Shimanami Kaido, a scenic cycling route across the Seto Inland Sea.

  • The southern starting point of the Shimanami Kaido cycling route.
  • A major shipbuilding and towel-manufacturing industry.
  • Dramatic bridge crossings between Seto Inland Sea islands.
Uchiko & OzuPreserved Craft Towns

Beautifully preserved historic towns known for traditional wax and paper crafts and a riverside reconstructed castle.

  • Uchiko's preserved townscape tied to traditional wax production.
  • Ozu Castle, a faithfully reconstructed wooden castle.
  • A quieter, traditional alternative to Matsuyama.
Uwajima & NanyoRemote South Coast

A remote southern coastal region known for a unique castle, bullfighting tradition, and scenic Uwa Sea aquaculture.

  • Uwajima Castle, one of Japan's original surviving castles.
  • A local tradition of togyu (bull-versus-bull sumo).
  • Scenic pearl and fish farming along the Uwa Sea coastline.
