Prefecture Area Guide

Osaka by Area

Osaka Prefecture breaks down into a handful of distinct areas, each with its own character. Click a pin on the map, or scroll down, to explore each one.

Where each area sits within Osaka
Osaka Prefecture's real boundary (same map data as the main site) — click a pin to jump to that area.
Central OsakaFood & Neon Capital

The city's commercial heart, centered on Dotonbori's neon-lit canal and the food-obsessed streets of Namba and Shinsaibashi.

  • Dotonbori's neon signs and canal-side food culture.
  • Namba and Shinsaibashi's dense shopping and dining streets.
  • Osaka's legendary street food, from takoyaki to okonomiyaki.
Osaka Castle & KitaCastle & Business District

Osaka Castle's iconic keep and park, alongside the Kita/Umeda business district with its skyscrapers and department stores.

  • Osaka Castle, one of Japan's most famous landmarks.
  • Umeda Sky Building's rooftop observation deck.
  • A major business and shopping hub around Osaka Station.
Bay AreaWaterfront & Theme Parks

Osaka's waterfront, home to Universal Studios Japan and the Kaiyukan aquarium, one of the world's largest.

  • Universal Studios Japan, a major international theme park.
  • Kaiyukan, one of the world's largest aquariums.
  • A large waterfront Ferris wheel and shopping complex.
Sakai & Southern OsakaAncient Kofun & Cutlery

A historic industrial city south of Osaka, home to ancient burial mounds (kofun) and a centuries-old cutlery-making tradition.

  • Nintoku-ryo Kofun, one of the world's largest ancient tombs by area.
  • A centuries-old tradition of fine cutlery and blade-making.
  • A quieter, historic contrast to central Osaka.
