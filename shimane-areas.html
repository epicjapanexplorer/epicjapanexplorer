Prefecture Area Guide

Shimane by Area

Shimane Prefecture breaks down into a handful of distinct areas, each with its own character. Click a pin on the map, or scroll down, to explore each one.

Where each area sits within Shimane
Shimane Prefecture's real boundary (same map data as the main site) — click a pin to jump to that area.
MatsueLakeside Castle Town

A castle town built around a network of lakes and canals, home to one of Japan's few original castles and a striking sunset over Lake Shinji.

  • Matsue Castle — one of Japan's few surviving original castles.
  • Canal boat tours through the historic castle town.
  • Renowned sunset views over Lake Shinji.
IzumoJapan's Ancient Shrine

Home to Izumo Taisha, one of Japan's oldest and most important shrines, deeply tied to Shinto mythology and matchmaking legend.

  • Izumo Taisha — one of Japan's oldest and most sacred shrines.
  • A massive shimenawa (sacred rope), among the largest in Japan.
  • Believed to be where Japan's gods gather each autumn.
Iwami GinzanUNESCO Silver Mine

A UNESCO World Heritage former silver mine that was once one of the largest in the world, set in a preserved mountain village.

  • A UNESCO World Heritage mining site, once a major global silver source.
  • Walkable mine tunnels open to visitors.
  • A preserved historic mining village.
Oki IslandsRemote Geopark Islands

A remote, rugged volcanic island group known for dramatic sea cliffs, Global Geopark status, and a slower island pace of life.

  • UNESCO Global Geopark status for its unique geology.
  • Dramatic sea cliffs and rugged coastal scenery.
  • A remote, slow-paced island atmosphere.
