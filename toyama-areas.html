Prefecture Area Guide

Toyama by Area

Toyama Prefecture breaks down into a handful of distinct areas, each with its own character. Click a pin on the map, or scroll down, to explore each one.

Where each area sits within Toyama
Toyama Prefecture's real boundary (same map data as the main site) — click a pin to jump to that area.
Toyama CityBay City & Alpine Views

The prefectural capital on Toyama Bay, known for its views of the snow-capped Tateyama mountains and fresh seafood, including prized white shrimp.

  • Views of the snow-capped Tateyama mountains across the bay.
  • Toyama Bay's prized white shrimp and winter yellowtail.
  • A modern, walkable city with a distinctive light-rail system.
Tateyama Kurobe Alpine RouteHigh Mountain Route

A spectacular high-mountain route crossing the Northern Alps, famous for its towering spring "Snow Corridor" walls.

  • The "Snow Corridor" — walls of snow up to 20m high each spring.
  • A combination of cable cars, ropeways, and buses across the Alps.
  • Some of Japan's most dramatic high-altitude mountain scenery.
TakaokaCasting & Bronze Craft Town

A historic casting and metalwork town, home to a great bronze Buddha statue and a well-preserved merchant district.

  • The Great Buddha of Takaoka, a celebrated bronze statue.
  • A 400-year tradition of bronze and metal casting.
  • Preserved Edo-period merchant and casters' districts.
GokayamaUNESCO Thatched Village

A remote mountain village of traditional steep thatched-roof houses, a quieter UNESCO World Heritage counterpart to nearby Shirakawa-go.

  • Steep thatched "gassho-zukuri" farmhouses, UNESCO World Heritage listed.
  • A quieter, less-crowded alternative to Shirakawa-go.
  • Remote mountain scenery, especially striking under winter snow.
Kurobe-Unazuki OnsenGorge Railway Onsen

A hot spring resort area at the entrance to the dramatic Kurobe Gorge, best known for the scenic Kurobe Gorge Railway trolley train ride through the canyon.

  • The Kurobe Gorge Railway — an open-air trolley train through a dramatic canyon.
  • Hot spring ryokan at the gorge's entrance.
  • Vivid autumn foliage along the gorge in October–November.
