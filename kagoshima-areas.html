Prefecture Area Guide

Kagoshima by Area

Kagoshima Prefecture breaks down into a handful of distinct areas, each with its own character. Click a pin on the map, or scroll down, to explore each one.

Where each area sits within Kagoshima
Kagoshima Prefecture's real boundary (same map data as the main site) — click a pin to jump to that area.
Kagoshima City & SakurajimaVolcano Across the Bay

The prefectural capital, set dramatically across the bay from Sakurajima, one of Japan's most active volcanoes.

  • Sakurajima, an active volcano visible across the bay.
  • Ferries connecting the city directly to Sakurajima.
  • A subtropical, southern Kyushu atmosphere.
IbusukiNatural Sand Baths

A coastal hot spring town famous for its natural sand baths, where visitors are buried in naturally heated volcanic sand.

  • Being buried up to the neck in naturally heated sand.
  • A unique, only-in-Ibusuki hot spring experience.
  • Coastal views along the Satsuma Peninsula.
KirishimaSacred Volcanic Range

A volcanic mountain range of shrines, hot springs, and hiking trails, tied to Japan's founding creation mythology.

  • Kirishima Jingu, a shrine tied to Japan's creation mythology.
  • Numerous hiking trails across the volcanic range.
  • Scattered hot spring villages throughout the mountains.
YakushimaUNESCO Ancient Forest

A UNESCO World Heritage island of ancient cedar forests, some trees thousands of years old, and lush, mossy rainforest trails.

  • Jomon Sugi and other cedar trees, some over 1,000 years old.
  • A UNESCO World Heritage mossy, misty rainforest landscape.
  • Said to have inspired scenery in Studio Ghibli's Princess Mononoke.
AmamiSubtropical Island Culture

A subtropical island known for coral reefs, mangrove forests, and a distinct culture blending mainland Japanese and Okinawan influences.

  • Coral reefs and clear water for snorkeling and diving.
  • Mangrove kayaking tours through coastal forests.
  • A distinct culture and dialect blending Japanese and Okinawan traditions.
