Prefecture Area Guide

Tokushima by Area

Tokushima Prefecture breaks down into a handful of distinct areas, each with its own character. Click a pin on the map, or scroll down, to explore each one.

Where each area sits within Tokushima
Tokushima Prefecture's real boundary (same map data as the main site) — click a pin to jump to that area.
Tokushima CityHome of Awa Odori

The prefectural capital, famous nationwide for the Awa Odori dance festival that fills its streets each August.

  • Awa Odori — one of Japan's largest and most famous dance festivals.
  • Year-round Awa Odori performances at a dedicated hall.
  • A riverside city center below Mt. Bizan.
NarutoTidal Whirlpools

Home to the Naruto Whirlpools, powerful tidal vortices in the strait between Tokushima and Awaji Island.

  • The Naruto Whirlpools, among the largest tidal vortices in the world.
  • Viewing boats and a glass-floored bridge walkway.
  • Dramatic tidal currents from the Seto Inland Sea meeting the Pacific.
Iya ValleyRemote Vine Bridges

A remote, steep mountain gorge known for vine bridges, hot springs, and dramatic valley scenery.

  • Historic vine suspension bridges over the gorge.
  • Remote hot spring inns tucked into the mountains.
  • Dramatic, steep valley scenery, one of Japan's most secluded regions.
