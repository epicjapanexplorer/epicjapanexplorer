Miyazaki Prefecture breaks down into a handful of distinct areas, each with its own character. Click a pin on the map, or scroll down, to explore each one.

Miyazaki City & AoshimaSubtropical Capital

The prefectural capital, known for its subtropical palm-lined streets and the small, mythic island of Aoshima just offshore.

  • Palm-lined streets giving the city a subtropical resort feel.
  • Aoshima, a small island ringed by unique "Devil's Washboard" rock.
  • A laid-back, sunny coastal atmosphere.
TakachihoMythic Gorge

A mythic gorge tied to Japan's ancient creation legends, famous for dramatic cliffs, waterfalls, and traditional nighttime kagura dance.

  • Takachiho Gorge's dramatic cliffs and Manai Falls.
  • Nightly kagura dance performances rooted in ancient myth.
  • Deep ties to Japan's Shinto creation mythology.
Cape ToiWild Horses

A remote, windswept cape known for wild horses roaming freely across its grassy coastal hills.

  • Wild horses (Misaki horses) roaming freely across the cape.
  • Sweeping, windswept coastal grassland views.
  • A remote, little-visited corner of Miyazaki.
