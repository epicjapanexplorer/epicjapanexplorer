About the Creator

Ryan Matsuno

Hi, I'm Ryan Matsuno, a Japanese travel creator based in Tokyo.

I have traveled to all 47 prefectures of Japan, exploring everything from iconic landmarks to hidden local gems. My mission is to help international travelers experience the authentic beauty, culture, and cuisine of Japan beyond the typical tourist routes.

Before becoming a travel content creator, I spent more than 20 years in marketing and brand management in Japan. That experience has shaped the way I present destinations—combining engaging storytelling with practical travel information.

Having lived in the United States and traveled extensively abroad, I understand what international visitors look for when exploring Japan.

Through this website and my YouTube channel, I share breathtaking landscapes, unforgettable local foods, hidden destinations, and practical travel tips to help you discover the very best of Japan.

Thank you for visiting Epic Japan Explorer. I hope my experiences inspire your next adventure in Japan.
