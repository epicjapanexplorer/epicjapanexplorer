Aomori City & AsamushiPrefectural Capital

The prefectural capital, home to the striking Nebuta Warasse museum celebrating the summer festival, with the quiet hot spring town of Asamushi just along the coast.

  • Nebuta Warasse — giant illuminated festival floats on permanent display.
  • Nebuta Festival (early August) — Aomori's explosive summer celebration.
  • Asamushi Onsen — a relaxed coastal hot spring town nearby.
HirosakiCastle & Cherry Blossoms

A castle town famous for one of Japan's most beautiful cherry blossom displays, set against Tsugaru-style apple orchards and a well-preserved Meiji-era streetscape.

  • Hirosaki Castle Park — widely considered Japan's best cherry blossom spot.
  • Apple orchards surrounding the city — Japan's top apple-growing region.
  • A charming mix of samurai-era and Western-style Meiji buildings.
Oirase GorgeForest Gorge

A serene forested river valley lined with waterfalls and mossy boulders, one of Japan's most celebrated walking trails, especially vivid in autumn.

  • A flat, easy 14km walking trail alongside the Oirase River.
  • Numerous named waterfalls along the route.
  • Adjacent to Lake Towada, a scenic caldera lake.
HachinoheFishing Port

A working fishing port known for its lively morning market and fresh seafood, plus the atmospheric Yokocho alleyway bars.

  • Hachinohe Morning Market — one of Japan's largest, Sundays only.
  • Miroku Yokocho — narrow lantern-lit alleys of small izakaya.
  • A working harbor with strong ties to Pacific fishing traditions.
