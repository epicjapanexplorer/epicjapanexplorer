Prefecture Area Guide

Kochi by Area

Kochi Prefecture breaks down into a handful of distinct areas, each with its own character. Click a pin on the map, or scroll down, to explore each one.

Where each area sits within Kochi
Kochi Prefecture's real boundary (same map data as the main site) — click a pin to jump to that area.
Kochi City & KatsurahamaRyoma's Hometown

The prefectural capital, home to an original castle and the scenic Katsurahama Beach, tied to the legendary samurai Sakamoto Ryoma.

  • Kochi Castle, one of Japan's few original castles.
  • Katsurahama Beach, with a statue of Sakamoto Ryoma.
  • A lively Sunday market tradition dating back centuries.
ShimantoJapan's Last Clear Stream

Home to the Shimanto River, often called Japan's last clear stream, known for its rustic low-water bridges.

  • Often called "Japan's last clear stream."
  • Chinka-bashi — rustic low-water bridges without railings.
  • Canoeing and river activities in a pristine setting.
Niyodo RiverVivid Blue Water

A river famous for producing some of the clearest, most vividly blue water in Japan.

  • Famous for its striking, vivid blue-green water color.
  • Popular swimming and photography spots along the river.
  • A quieter, less-visited natural attraction.
KashiwajimaCrystal-Clear Snorkeling

A small island known for exceptionally clear waters and some of Japan's best snorkeling and diving.

  • Some of Japan's clearest waters for snorkeling and diving.
  • Rich coral and marine life despite its temperate latitude.
  • A remote, small-scale island destination.
