Prefecture Area Guide

Hyogo by Area

Hyogo Prefecture breaks down into a handful of distinct areas, each with its own character. Click a pin on the map, or scroll down, to explore each one.

Where each area sits within Hyogo
Hyogo Prefecture's real boundary (same map data as the main site) — click a pin to jump to that area.
KobePort City & Wagyu

A cosmopolitan port city known for its harbor views, Chinatown, and the prized Kobe beef that bears its name.

  • Kobe beef, one of the world's most celebrated wagyu brands.
  • Kobe Harborland, a scenic waterfront shopping and dining area.
  • Nankinmachi, Kobe's compact Chinatown district.
HimejiUNESCO Original Castle

Home to Himeji Castle, Japan's most spectacular original castle and a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

  • Himeji Castle — Japan's most magnificent original castle.
  • A UNESCO World Heritage Site nicknamed the "White Heron Castle."
  • One of the best-preserved feudal-era castle complexes in Japan.
Awaji IslandIsland of Onions & Flowers

A large island in the Seto Inland Sea known for onions, flower parks, and dramatic bridge views connecting it to the mainland.

  • Awaji onions, famous throughout Japan for their sweetness.
  • Large seasonal flower parks across the island.
  • Views of the Akashi Kaikyo Bridge, one of the world's longest suspension bridges.
Kinosaki & TajimaYukata Hot Spring Town

A classic hot spring town famous for yukata-clad strolls between bathhouses, in Hyogo's quieter northern countryside.

  • Strolling between seven public bathhouses in a yukata robe.
  • A willow-lined canal running through the town center.
  • A relaxed, quieter part of Hyogo's northern coast.
