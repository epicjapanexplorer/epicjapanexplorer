Prefecture Area Guide

Shizuoka by Area

Shizuoka Prefecture breaks down into a handful of distinct areas, each with its own character. Click a pin on the map, or scroll down, to explore each one.

Where each area sits within Shizuoka
Shizuoka Prefecture's real boundary (same map data as the main site) — click a pin to jump to that area.
IzuPeninsula Getaway

A scenic peninsula of hot springs, dramatic coastlines, and seaside resort towns, long favored as a nearby escape from Tokyo.

  • Numerous hot spring resort towns along the coast.
  • Dramatic rocky coastlines and beaches.
  • Easy access from Tokyo, a popular weekend getaway.
Mt. Fuji & GotembaMt. Fuji Views

Home to some of the closest and most dramatic views of Mt. Fuji, plus a major outlet shopping destination at its base.

  • Some of the closest, most dramatic views of Mt. Fuji.
  • Gotemba Premium Outlets, one of Japan's largest outlet malls.
  • Access to Mt. Fuji's 5th Station climbing routes.
HamamatsuInstruments & Industry

A major industrial city on the Pacific coast, known as the birthplace of Japanese musical instrument and motorcycle manufacturing.

  • Birthplace of Yamaha and other major instrument makers.
  • A significant motorcycle and auto manufacturing heritage.
  • Hamamatsu Museum of Musical Instruments.
AtamiClassic Seaside Onsen

A classic hot spring resort town on Sagami Bay, known for its ocean views, summer fireworks festivals, and retro seaside charm.

  • Frequent fireworks festivals over Sagami Bay in summer.
  • A retro, nostalgic seaside hot spring resort atmosphere.
  • Easy access from Tokyo via shinkansen.
