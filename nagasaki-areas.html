Prefecture Area Guide

Nagasaki by Area

Nagasaki Prefecture breaks down into a handful of distinct areas, each with its own character. Click a pin on the map, or scroll down, to explore each one.

Where each area sits within Nagasaki
Nagasaki Prefecture's real boundary (same map data as the main site) — click a pin to jump to that area.
Nagasaki CityEast-Meets-West Port

A historic port city with a unique blend of Japanese, Chinese, and Western heritage, and a poignant atomic bomb history.

  • Glover Garden's Western-style Meiji-era mansions.
  • A historic Chinatown and Dutch trading heritage.
  • The Atomic Bomb Museum and Peace Park.
SaseboGateway to '99 Islands'

A naval port city and gateway to the scenic Kujukushima ("99 Islands") coastline.

  • Kujukushima — a scenic scatter of small islands along the coast.
  • A historic naval port with U.S. and Japanese naval bases.
  • Huis Ten Bosch, a large Dutch-themed park nearby.
Unzen & ShimabaraVolcanic Hot Springs

A volcanic hot spring resort area beside Mt. Unzen, with a dramatic history of eruption and recovery.

  • Unzen Jigoku — steaming volcanic hot spring vents.
  • A dramatic modern volcanic eruption history (1990s).
  • Shimabara's castle town and clear spring-fed canals.
Goto IslandsRemote Hidden Christian Heritage

A remote archipelago known for pristine beaches, hidden Christian heritage sites, and a slower, untouched island atmosphere.

  • UNESCO World Heritage hidden Christian sites and churches.
  • Pristine, little-visited beaches.
  • A remote, slow-paced island chain far off the mainland.
