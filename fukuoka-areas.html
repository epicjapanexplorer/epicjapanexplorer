Fukuoka Prefecture breaks down into a handful of distinct areas, each with its own character. Click a pin on the map, or scroll down, to explore each one.

Fukuoka CityKyushu's Largest City

Kyushu's largest city, known for its yatai food stalls, Hakata ramen, and a lively blend of history and modern urban life.

  • Yatai — open-air food stalls serving ramen and local dishes.
  • Hakata-style tonkotsu ramen, a Fukuoka specialty.
  • A modern, walkable city with a strong food culture.
DazaifuShrine of Scholarship

Home to Dazaifu Tenmangu, one of Japan's most important shrines dedicated to the god of scholarship.

  • Dazaifu Tenmangu, a major shrine for academic success.
  • A lively approach street of traditional sweets and shops.
  • Kyushu National Museum nearby.
ItoshimaCoastal Cafe Peninsula

A scenic coastal peninsula popular for beaches, cafes, and photogenic shrine gates set against the sea.

  • Sakurai Futamigaura's iconic torii gate set in the sea.
  • A growing scene of beachside cafes and shops.
  • Popular beaches within easy reach of Fukuoka City.
YanagawaCanal Boat Town

A canal town known for traditional boat punting tours through its historic waterways.

  • Traditional punted boat tours through the town's canals.
  • A historic castle-town canal network.
  • Local eel cuisine, a Yanagawa specialty.
