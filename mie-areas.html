Mie by Area

Mie Prefecture breaks down into a handful of distinct areas, each with its own character. Click a pin on the map, or scroll down, to explore each one.

Where each area sits within Mie
Mie Prefecture's real boundary (same map data as the main site) — click a pin to jump to that area.
IseShinto's Spiritual Heart

Home to Ise Jingu, the most sacred shrine in Shinto and spiritual heart of Japan, along with the lively Okage Yokocho food street.

  • Ise Jingu — the most sacred shrine complex in Shinto.
  • Okage Yokocho, a lively street of food stalls near the shrine.
  • A tradition of rebuilding the shrine anew every 20 years.
Shima & TobaPearls & Coastline

A scenic coastal region known for pearl cultivation, Ise-Shima National Park, and fresh seafood from Ago Bay.

  • The birthplace of Japan's cultured pearl industry.
  • Ise-Shima National Park's scenic coastline.
  • Fresh seafood, especially from Ago Bay.
KumanoSacred Pilgrimage Region

A remote, mountainous pilgrimage region on the Kumano Kodo trail, with sacred waterfalls and ancient shrines.

  • Part of the historic Kumano Kodo pilgrimage trail network.
  • Remote, mountainous, and rich in ancient spiritual sites.
  • A quieter, less-visited corner of Mie.
