Takayama"Little Kyoto"

A beautifully preserved mountain town often called "Little Kyoto," known for its Edo-period merchant streets, morning markets, and local sake breweries.

  • Sanmachi Suji — preserved Edo-period merchant streets.
  • Two lively morning markets held daily along the river and shrine grounds.
  • Local sake breweries marked by distinctive cedar-ball signs.
Shirakawa-goUNESCO Thatched Village

A UNESCO World Heritage village of steep thatched-roof "gassho-zukuri" farmhouses, especially magical when lit up under winter snow.

  • Dozens of steep thatched-roof farmhouses, some open as museums.
  • A viewpoint overlooking the whole village from the hillside above.
  • Special winter light-up nights, hugely popular and reservation-only.
Gero OnsenOne of Japan's Top Onsen

One of Japan's three most celebrated hot spring towns, a relaxed riverside resort known for its smooth, skin-softening water.

  • Ranked among Japan's three greatest hot spring towns for centuries.
  • Free public foot baths scattered around town.
  • A relaxed riverside atmosphere, easy to explore on foot.
