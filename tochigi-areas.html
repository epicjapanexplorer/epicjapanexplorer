Prefecture Area Guide

Tochigi by Area

Tochigi Prefecture breaks down into a handful of distinct areas, each with its own character. Click a pin on the map, or scroll down, to explore each one.

Where each area sits within Tochigi
Tochigi Prefecture's real boundary (same map data as the main site) — click a pin to jump to that area.
NikkoUNESCO Shrine Town

A UNESCO World Heritage mountain town famous for the lavishly decorated Toshogu Shrine, dramatic waterfalls, and some of Japan's finest autumn colors.

  • Toshogu Shrine — elaborately carved, gilded UNESCO World Heritage shrine.
  • Kegon Falls — a dramatic 97m waterfall near Lake Chuzenji.
  • Irohazaka — a winding mountain road famous for autumn foliage.
NasuHighland Retreat

A cool highland resort area popular for hot springs, dairy farms, and hiking, long favored as a retreat by Japan's imperial family.

  • Nasu Imperial Villa grounds, partly open to the public.
  • Dairy farms and cheese producers dotting the highlands.
  • Cooler summer temperatures than the Kanto plain below.
Kinugawa OnsenRiver Gorge Onsen

A riverside hot spring resort town near Nikko, known for its gorge views and large ryokan hotels — a popular base for exploring the area.

  • Hot spring ryokan overlooking the Kinugawa gorge.
  • A convenient base for day trips to Nikko.
  • Boat rides through the river gorge in season.
UtsunomiyaJapan's Gyoza Capital

The prefectural capital, nationally famous as Japan's "gyoza (dumpling) capital," with dozens of specialist restaurants around the station.

  • Gyoza Stadium and dozens of specialist shops citywide.
  • A modern, easy-to-navigate prefectural capital.
  • Good rail access as a gateway to the rest of Tochigi.
AshikagaWisteria & Old School

Home to the Ashikaga Flower Park, famous for its spectacular wisteria tunnels each spring, and one of Japan's oldest schools, Ashikaga Gakko.

  • Ashikaga Flower Park — legendary wisteria tunnels, late April–May.
  • Ashikaga Gakko — one of Japan's oldest schools, dating back centuries.
  • A quieter, less-visited alternative to nearby tourist hubs.
