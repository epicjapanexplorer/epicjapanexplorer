Prefecture Area Guide

Kumamoto by Area

Kumamoto Prefecture breaks down into a handful of distinct areas, each with its own character. Click a pin on the map, or scroll down, to explore each one.

Where each area sits within Kumamoto
Kumamoto Prefecture's real boundary (same map data as the main site) — click a pin to jump to that area.
Kumamoto CityCastle & Kumamon

Home to Kumamoto Castle, one of Japan's most formidable feudal fortresses, along with the beloved mascot bear Kumamon.

  • Kumamoto Castle, one of Japan's three great castles.
  • Kumamon, the wildly popular prefectural mascot.
  • Suizenji Jojuen, a garden modeled after the old Tokaido road.
AsoGiant Volcanic Caldera

Home to one of the world's largest volcanic calderas, with vast grasslands, an active crater, and dramatic panoramic views.

  • One of the world's largest volcanic calderas.
  • An active crater visitors can approach when conditions allow.
  • Vast grasslands used for traditional cattle grazing.
AmakusaIslands & Dolphins

A scattered island region known for dolphin watching, hidden Christian heritage, and a mild, relaxed coastal atmosphere.

  • Wild dolphin watching tours, a signature local activity.
  • UNESCO-linked hidden Christian heritage sites.
  • A relaxed, scenic island-hopping atmosphere.
