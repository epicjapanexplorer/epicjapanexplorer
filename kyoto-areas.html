Prefecture Area Guide

Kyoto by Area

Kyoto Prefecture breaks down into a handful of distinct areas, each with its own character. Click a pin on the map, or scroll down, to explore each one.

Where each area sits within Kyoto
Kyoto Prefecture's real boundary (same map data as the main site) — click a pin to jump to that area.
Central KyotoThe Historic Capital's Heart

The heart of the historic capital, home to Kinkaku-ji, Kiyomizu-dera, Gion's geisha district, and countless other landmark temples and shrines.

  • Kinkaku-ji — the iconic Golden Pavilion.
  • Kiyomizu-dera — a UNESCO World Heritage wooden temple.
  • Gion, Kyoto's famous geisha and geiko entertainment district.
ArashiyamaBamboo Grove District

A scenic western district famous for its towering bamboo grove, a historic bridge, and mountainside temples.

  • The Arashiyama Bamboo Grove — a towering, otherworldly path.
  • Togetsukyo Bridge, a scenic landmark over the Katsura River.
  • Mountainside temples with sweeping valley views.
Fushimi & UjiTorii Gates & Tea Culture

Home to the iconic thousand-torii-gate Fushimi Inari Shrine, and Uji, the birthplace of Japanese green tea culture.

  • Fushimi Inari — thousands of vermillion torii gates up the mountain.
  • Uji, the historic birthplace of Japanese green tea.
  • Byodo-in Temple, depicted on the 10-yen coin.
Amanohashidate & IneOne of Japan's Three Great Views

A dramatic pine-covered sandbar ranked among Japan's three great scenic views, near Ine's unique boathouse fishing village.

  • A 3.6km pine-covered sandbar, one of Japan's three great views.
  • The famous "viewing between your legs" vantage point.
  • Ine's funaya — traditional boathouses built right over the water.
