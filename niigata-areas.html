Prefecture Area Guide

Niigata by Area

Niigata Prefecture breaks down into a handful of distinct areas, each with its own character. Click a pin on the map, or scroll down, to explore each one.

Where each area sits within Niigata
Niigata Prefecture's real boundary (same map data as the main site) — click a pin to jump to that area.
Niigata CitySea of Japan Port

The prefectural capital and a major Sea of Japan port, known for its rice, sake breweries, and riverside cityscape.

  • Numerous local sake breweries open for tasting.
  • Furumachi and other historic geisha districts.
  • Bandai Bridge and a scenic riverside downtown.
Sado IslandRemote Island Heritage

A remote, history-rich island once used for gold mining and political exile, known today for traditional taiko drumming and dramatic coastal scenery.

  • Sado Kinzan — a historic gold mine open for tours.
  • Home of Kodo, one of the world's leading taiko drumming ensembles.
  • Dramatic coastal scenery and traditional tub-boat rides.
Echigo-YuzawaSki Resort & "Snow Country"

A renowned ski resort town easily reached by shinkansen from Tokyo, also famous as the setting for Kawabata's novel "Snow Country."

  • Major ski resorts just an hour from Tokyo by shinkansen.
  • The real-world setting for Kawabata's Nobel-linked novel "Snow Country."
  • Hot springs to soak in after a day on the slopes.
Tsubame-SanjoMetalwork Craft City

A twin-city manufacturing hub famous nationwide for its metalwork craftsmanship, from tableware to precision tools.

  • Renowned metalworking and tableware craftsmanship.
  • Factory tours and workshops at local metalwork studios.
  • A working craft-industry town rather than a typical tourist stop.
Murakami & Senami OnsenCastle Town & Salmon Culture

A historic castle town near the coast, known for salmon culture and cuisine, paired with the nearby Senami hot spring resort on the Sea of Japan.

  • A centuries-old salmon fishing and preservation culture.
  • A preserved merchant townscape near the old castle site.
  • Senami Onsen, a coastal hot spring resort nearby.
