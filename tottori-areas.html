Tottori by Area

Tottori Prefecture breaks down into a handful of distinct areas, each with its own character. Click a pin on the map, or scroll down, to explore each one.

Where each area sits within Tottori
Tottori Prefecture's real boundary (same map data as the main site) — click a pin to jump to that area.
Tottori Sand Dunes & Tottori CityJapan's Great Dunes

Japan's only large-scale desert-like dune system, stretching along the Sea of Japan coast beside the prefectural capital.

  • Japan's largest and most famous sand dune system.
  • Camel rides and paragliding across the dunes.
  • The Sand Museum, with intricate large-scale sand sculptures.
Kurayoshi & MisasaWhite-Walled Town & Onsen

A preserved white-walled merchant townscape paired with Misasa Onsen, known for some of the most radioactive (and reputedly therapeutic) spring water in the world.

  • Kurayoshi's preserved white-walled storehouse district.
  • Misasa Onsen, noted for its unusually radioactive spring water.
  • A quieter, traditional corner of Tottori.
Mt. DaisenChugoku's Tallest Peak

The tallest mountain in the Chugoku region, offering hiking, skiing, and a dramatic profile often compared to Mt. Fuji.

  • The tallest mountain in the Chugoku region.
  • A dramatic profile often nicknamed "Izumo-Fuji."
  • Hiking in summer, skiing in winter.
Sakaiminato & YonagoManga Port Town

A quirky port town famous for its Kitaro manga-character statues, paired with the nearby transit hub of Yonago.

  • Mizuki Shigeru Road, lined with beloved yokai character statues.
  • A working fishing port with fresh seafood.
  • Yonago, the area's main transit hub.
