Prefecture Area Guide

Chiba by Area

Chiba Prefecture breaks down into a handful of distinct areas, each with its own character. Click a pin on the map, or scroll down, to explore each one.

Where each area sits within Chiba
Chiba Prefecture's real boundary (same map data as the main site) — click a pin to jump to that area.
MaihamaTheme Park Resort

Home to Tokyo Disney Resort — Tokyo Disneyland and DisneySea — one of the most-visited theme park destinations in the world.

  • Tokyo Disneyland — the classic Disney theme park experience.
  • Tokyo DisneySea — a unique, ocean-themed park found only in Japan.
  • Directly connected to Tokyo by train, an easy day trip.
NaritaAirport & Temple Town

Best known for Narita International Airport, but also home to Naritasan Shinshoji, a major Buddhist temple with a lively approach street of shops and eateries.

  • Naritasan Shinshoji — a major temple founded over 1,000 years ago.
  • Omotesando approach street, famous for grilled eel (unagi) restaurants.
  • A convenient stopover given its proximity to Narita Airport.
KisarazuBay Pier & Outlets

A Tokyo Bay city known for its long pier stretching into the water and as a gateway to the Boso Peninsula's outlet malls.

  • Nakanose no Tori-i — a striking torii gate rising from the bay at low tide.
  • Mitsui Outlet Park, one of the region's largest outlet malls.
  • Easy access via the Tokyo Bay Aqua-Line.
KujukuriPacific Coast Beach

A dramatic, nearly 60km-long sandy beach on the Pacific coast, popular for surfing and summer beach outings.

  • Nearly 60km of continuous sandy beach.
  • A top surfing destination in the Kanto region.
  • Wide open Pacific views, especially striking at sunrise.
Minami-BosoFlower Coast

The southern tip of the Boso Peninsula, known for its mild climate, flower fields that bloom even in winter, and laid-back coastal scenery.

  • Flower fields blooming even in winter thanks to the mild climate.
  • A laid-back, less-touristed stretch of coastline.
  • Fresh seafood from the surrounding fishing communities.
