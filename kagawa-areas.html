Prefecture Area Guide

Kagawa by Area

Kagawa Prefecture breaks down into a handful of distinct areas, each with its own character. Click a pin on the map, or scroll down, to explore each one.

Where each area sits within Kagawa
Kagawa Prefecture's real boundary (same map data as the main site) — click a pin to jump to that area.
TakamatsuUdon Prefecture's Capital

The prefectural capital, home to Ritsurin Garden — one of Japan's most celebrated landscape gardens — and famously Japan's "Udon Prefecture" hub.

  • Ritsurin Garden, among Japan's most celebrated landscape gardens.
  • Countless local udon shops, a point of regional pride.
  • A gateway port to the Seto Inland Sea islands.
Naoshima & TeshimaContemporary Art Islands

Art-focused islands in the Seto Inland Sea, home to major contemporary art museums, installations, and architecture by Tadao Ando.

  • Benesse House and the Chichu Art Museum, designed by Tadao Ando.
  • Yayoi Kusama's iconic giant pumpkin sculpture.
  • Teshima Art Museum's striking minimalist architecture.
ShodoshimaOlive Island

A scenic island known for olive groves, soy sauce breweries, and a dramatic gorge often compared to Greece.

  • Japan's first and largest olive groves.
  • Traditional soy sauce breweries open for tours.
  • Kankakei Gorge, one of Japan's three great gorges.
KotohiraKonpira-san's Stone Steps

Home to Kotohira-gu (Konpira-san), a mountainside shrine reached by a famous, steep 1,368-step stone staircase.

  • A famous 1,368-step climb to the main shrine.
  • Long revered by sailors as a shrine of maritime safety.
  • Sweeping views over the Sanuki plain from the summit.
