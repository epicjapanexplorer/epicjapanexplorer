Oita Prefecture breaks down into a handful of distinct areas, each with its own character. Click a pin on the map, or scroll down, to explore each one.

BeppuJapan's Top Hot Spring City

Japan's top hot spring destination by volume, famous for its "hells" — vividly colored, steaming scenic hot springs not meant for bathing.

  • The "Beppu Hells" — vividly colored scenic hot springs.
  • Japan's largest hot spring water output by volume.
  • Sand baths and mud baths among its many bathing styles.
YufuinBoutique Onsen Town

A refined hot spring resort town at the foot of Mt. Yufu, known for boutique ryokan, cafes, and art galleries.

  • A refined, boutique take on the classic hot spring town.
  • Mt. Yufu's scenic backdrop.
  • Art galleries, craft shops, and stylish cafes.
Hita & Yaba-KeiMerchant Town & Gorge

A preserved Edo-period merchant town paired with Yaba-Kei, a dramatic gorge of layered volcanic rock formations.

  • Hita's preserved Edo-period merchant streets.
  • Yaba-Kei's dramatic layered volcanic rock cliffs.
  • A quieter, scenic corner of inland Oita.
