Prefecture Area Guide

Ishikawa by Area

Ishikawa Prefecture breaks down into a handful of distinct areas, each with its own character. Click a pin on the map, or scroll down, to explore each one.

Where each area sits within Ishikawa
Ishikawa Prefecture's real boundary (same map data as the main site) — click a pin to jump to that area.
KanazawaCastle City & Gardens

A beautifully preserved castle city with one of Japan's three great gardens, a historic geisha district, and a renowned contemporary art museum.

  • Kenroku-en — one of Japan's three great gardens.
  • Higashi Chaya, a preserved geisha entertainment district.
  • 21st Century Museum of Contemporary Art, a major architectural draw.
Noto PeninsulaRugged Coastal Peninsula

A rugged, rural peninsula known for dramatic coastal scenery, terraced rice paddies by the sea, and traditional fishing villages.

  • Shiroyone Senmaida — terraced rice paddies cascading to the sea.
  • Dramatic rock formations along the rugged coastline.
  • Traditional fishing villages and a slower pace of life.
Kaga OnsenRefined Hot Spring Towns

A cluster of historic hot spring towns known for refined ryokan, artistic craft traditions, and mountain scenery.

  • A cluster of four distinct historic hot spring towns.
  • Refined ryokan culture and Kutani-yaki porcelain craft traditions.
  • A quieter, more traditional alternative to Kanazawa.
