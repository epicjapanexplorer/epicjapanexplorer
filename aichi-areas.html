Aichi by Area

Aichi Prefecture breaks down into a handful of distinct areas, each with its own character. Click a pin on the map, or scroll down, to explore each one.

Where each area sits within Aichi
Aichi Prefecture's real boundary (same map data as the main site) — click a pin to jump to that area.
NagoyaJapan's 4th-Largest City

Japan's fourth-largest city, known for its reconstructed castle, distinctive local cuisine, and major automotive industry heritage.

  • Nagoya Castle, with its famous golden shachihoko roof ornaments.
  • Distinctive local dishes like miso katsu and hitsumabushi.
  • Home to Toyota and a major center of Japan's auto industry.
InuyamaOriginal Castle on a River

Home to one of Japan's oldest original castles, perched above the Kiso River, with a charming traditional castle-town street below.

  • Inuyama Castle — one of Japan's oldest surviving original castles.
  • A charming preserved castle-town shopping street.
  • Traditional cormorant fishing on the Kiso River in summer.
Chita PeninsulaCoastal Peninsula

A coastal peninsula known for seafood, hot springs, and a more relaxed pace just outside central Nagoya.

  • Fresh seafood from the surrounding waters.
  • A handful of relaxed hot spring towns.
  • An easy, quieter contrast to central Nagoya.
