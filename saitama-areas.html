Prefecture Area Guide

Saitama by Area

Saitama Prefecture breaks down into a handful of distinct areas, each with its own character. Click a pin on the map, or scroll down, to explore each one.

Where each area sits within Saitama
Saitama Prefecture's real boundary (same map data as the main site) — click a pin to jump to that area.
Kawagoe"Little Edo"

Known as "Little Edo" for its preserved storehouse district and bell tower, a popular and easy day trip from Tokyo.

  • Kurazukuri storehouse district — Edo-period merchant architecture.
  • Toki no Kane, a historic wooden bell tower and city symbol.
  • Candy Alley (Kashiya Yokocho), lined with traditional sweet shops.
ChichibuMountains & Moss Phlox

A mountain-ringed area famous for shibazakura (moss phlox) fields, a lively night festival, and scenic river gorges.

  • Shibazakura Hill — vivid moss phlox fields each spring.
  • Chichibu Night Festival, one of Japan's three great float festivals.
  • Nagatoro Gorge for gentle river rafting.
OmiyaRailway & Shrine

Home to one of Japan's largest railway museums and Hikawa Shrine, one of the region's oldest and most revered.

  • The Railway Museum — one of Japan's largest, with historic locomotives.
  • Hikawa Shrine, approached by a long, tree-lined pathway.
  • A major rail hub with easy access across the Kanto region.
NagatoroRiver Gorge

A scenic river valley known for gentle rafting trips past dramatic rock formations along the Arakawa River.

  • Gentle rafting trips down the Arakawa River.
  • Iwadatami — dramatic layered rock formations along the riverbank.
  • A popular easy nature escape from central Saitama.
