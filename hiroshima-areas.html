Hiroshima by Area

Hiroshima Prefecture breaks down into a handful of distinct areas, each with its own character. Click a pin on the map, or scroll down, to explore each one.

Where each area sits within Hiroshima
Hiroshima Prefecture's real boundary (same map data as the main site) — click a pin to jump to that area.
Hiroshima CityPeace Memorial City

A city rebuilt around peace and remembrance, home to the Peace Memorial Park and Atomic Bomb Dome, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

  • The Atomic Bomb Dome, a UNESCO World Heritage peace memorial.
  • Peace Memorial Park and Museum.
  • A modern, resilient city center rebuilt after 1945.
MiyajimaFloating Torii Gate

A sacred island famous for Itsukushima Shrine's "floating" red torii gate and free-roaming deer.

  • Itsukushima Shrine's iconic torii gate, appearing to float at high tide.
  • Free-roaming deer throughout the island.
  • A ropeway up Mt. Misen for panoramic views.
Kure & EtajimaNaval Port Heritage

A historic naval port city with a rich maritime heritage, paired with the scenic island of Etajima nearby.

  • A historic Imperial Japanese Navy shipbuilding heritage.
  • The Yamato Museum, dedicated to the famous battleship.
  • Etajima Island's scenic, quieter coastal roads.
OnomichiHillside Temple Town

A hillside port town of narrow lanes and temples, known as the starting point of the scenic Shimanami Kaido cycling route.

  • A hillside "Temple Walk" through narrow, atmospheric lanes.
  • The starting point of the Shimanami Kaido cycling route.
  • A laid-back port-town atmosphere overlooking the Seto Inland Sea.
