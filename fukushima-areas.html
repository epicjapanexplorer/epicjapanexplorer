Fukushima by Area

Fukushima Prefecture breaks down into a handful of distinct areas, each with its own character. Click a pin on the map, or scroll down, to explore each one.

AizuwakamatsuCastle & Samurai History

A castle town steeped in samurai history, home to the rebuilt Tsuruga Castle and the tragic story of the Byakkotai youth corps.

  • Tsuruga Castle — a rebuilt keep with a red-tiled roof, rare in Japan.
  • Iimoriyama — site of the Byakkotai youth corps' last stand.
  • Preserved samurai residences around the old castle town.
KitakataRamen & Sake Warehouses

A small town famous nationwide for its ramen — with more shops per capita than almost anywhere in Japan — and its rows of traditional sake-brewery warehouses.

  • Dozens of famed ramen shops packed into a small town.
  • Traditional black-tiled sake-brewery warehouses (kura).
  • A popular day trip paired with nearby Aizuwakamatsu.
Bandai & InawashiroLake & Highlands

A highland resort area centered on Lake Inawashiro and Mt. Bandai, offering hiking, hot springs, and some of Tohoku's best autumn foliage.

  • Lake Inawashiro — one of Japan's largest lakes, with clear water.
  • Mt. Bandai — dramatic volcanic scenery and hiking trails.
  • Goshikinuma "Five Colored Ponds" — vividly colored volcanic pools.
